# Simplified Chinese glossary

The terms the Simplified Chinese pages use. Written by us; a native speaker reviews it with the pages.

## What stays in English

- The product name: Statscraft.
- Everything the reader types or sees in the program: command names and keywords (`FREQUENCIES`, `CTABLES`, `/TABLE`), the syntax, flags (`--json`), file names, variable names, code and code blocks.
- Menu items, buttons, tabs and dialog names, written in bold as in the English pages (**File > Open data...**), because the window is in English. A Chinese gloss may follow in parentheses where it helps.
- Names of other programs and formats: Excel, Word, PowerPoint, PDF, CSV, JSON, Markdown, MCP, SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, Dolt, Stata, SAS, PSPP, Claude, Codex, WSL.

## Style

- Full-width Chinese punctuation in Chinese sentences: ，。：；（）“”.
- One half-width space between Chinese and Latin text, numbers or code: 运行 `FREQUENCIES` 命令，共 300 个个案。
- No dash characters as punctuation; rewrite the sentence instead.
- Address the reader as 您.
